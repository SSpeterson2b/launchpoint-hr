How to Read LaunchPoint

LaunchPoint Score is the model's overall home-run matchup score. Daily Percentile shows where a player ranks versus the full daily player pool. Generator Score combines the model's power, matchup and opportunity signals. Trend Change shows whether a player's daily position is rising or falling.

Model Breakdown

The model evaluates Statcast Power, Pitch Arsenal matchup, Pitcher Vulnerability, Recent Form, Bullpen Vulnerability, Park + Weather, Lineup Opportunity and Handedness.

About LaunchPoint

LaunchPoint HR ranks daily home-run candidates using matchup quality, batted-ball power indicators, opposing pitching conditions and lineup opportunity. The board is designed to surface both strong targets and value or long-shot profiles.

Disclaimer

LaunchPoint projections are informational and do not guarantee outcomes. Baseball results are volatile and home runs remain difficult to predict.
